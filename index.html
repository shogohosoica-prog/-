How to play

Pick Addition or Subtraction, then choose a number to practice. You'll get nine quick questions.

Every fact you answer correctly becomes a light blue star. Come back on a different day and answer it right again, and it turns gold — perfect!

There's no rush and no login. Everything is saved automatically just for you.

Add Number Sky to your Home Screen so your stars are always saved and it opens full-screen, like an app.

Add to Home Screen

1. Tap the Share icon in Safari's toolbar (the square with an arrow pointing up).

2. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.

3. Tap Add. A "Number Sky" icon will appear on your Home Screen — open it from there from now on.

This keeps your saved stars safe even if Safari clears its history or storage.
