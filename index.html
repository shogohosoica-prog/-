How to play

Pick Addition or Subtraction, then choose a number to practice. You'll get ten quick questions, and a row of dots grouped in fives to help you see the numbers as you work it out.

Every fact you answer correctly becomes a light blue star. Come back on a different day and answer it right again, and it turns gold — perfect!

There's no rush and no login. Everything is saved automatically just for you.
